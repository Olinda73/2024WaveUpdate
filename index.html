Modern sounds with a classic rock philosophy

Who are we?

Wave Vendors uses older instruments, while using hand coded software for the recording process. We then run those digital recordings through various analog devices to give it the character of a classic record.

How it started?

A blending of the digital and analog audio worlds into a single vision, harnessing the strengths of each while complimenting each other to create a balanced and provocative sound. Tape decks and Ableton Live working in harmony.

What we stand for

A portion of all royalties made has been regularly donated to arts-related charities and nonprofits so that real creative skills can still be passed along in today's age of 'copy and paste' entertainment.

A.I. is not used by Wave Vendors
