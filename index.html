Who are we?

Wave Vendors uses older instruments, while using hand coded software for the recording process. We then run those digital recordings through various analog devices to give it the character and texture classic recordings are known for.

How it started?

A blending of the digital and analog audio worlds into a single vision, harnessing the strengths of each while complimenting each other to create a balanced and provocative sound. Tapestries of tone and texture help bring the music to life.
